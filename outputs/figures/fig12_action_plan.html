Methodology & assumptions ›

Every number on this page is computed deterministically from your inputs and the following assumptions. No machine learning, no API calls, no data leaves your browser.

  • Equity return assumption: 6.5% real (long-run S&P 500 net of inflation)
  • Bond return assumption: 2.0% real
  • Inflation: 2.5% (CPI long-run trend)
  • Mortgage rate: 7.0% (current 30-year fixed; updated quarterly)
  • Tuition growth: 4.5%/year (College Board historical)
  • Property tax: 1.2%/year of home value (US average)
  • Home maintenance: 1.5%/year of home value
  • HSA contribution limits: 2024 IRS, $4,300 single / $8,550 family
  • Dependent care FSA limit: $5,000 (IRS, 2024)
  • IRA contribution limit: $7,000 (IRS, 2024)
  • Roth IRA phase-out: $161K single / $240K married (2024)
  • Social Security replacement: ~40% of pre-retirement income
  • Safe withdrawal rate: 4% (Trinity / Bengen)
  • Childcare cost source: Child Care Aware of America, 2024 state averages
  • Pre-K eligibility ages: NIEER State of Preschool Yearbook 2024
  • State 529 deductions: state revenue department websites, 2024
  • Metro home prices: Zillow ZHVI, August 2024
  • Metro rents: Zillow ZORI 2-bedroom, August 2024
All calculations happen in your browser. No data is sent to any server. Refresh the page to clear your inputs.
